About Us

ABOUT US

Trading plastics with purpose, precision & trust

At Noor Pack UAE, we specialize in delivering high-quality customized packaging solutions that help businesses create a lasting impression. With a focus on quality, creativity, and reliability, we provide a wide range of packaging products tailored to meet the unique branding needs of businesses across the UAE.

Our product range includes premium tissue papers, custom kraft paper bags, custom plastic carry bags, packaging boxes, labels, and other branded packaging materials. We work closely with retailers, restaurants, cafés, bakeries, fashion brands, cosmetics companies, and corporate businesses to create packaging that reflects their brand identity.

Driven by innovation and customer satisfaction, Noor Pack UAE is committed to providing durable, attractive, and cost-effective packaging solutions that enhance brand visibility and elevate the customer experience.
